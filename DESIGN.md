# Nutriweave Design System

## Direction

Nutriweave uses the custom Sunlit Harvest system: a warm, colorful consumer-food identity inspired by the energy and product focus of the user-provided Lundberg reference, without copying its assets or content. The interface should feel optimistic, useful, and rooted in food.

## Material

- Warm rice-paper cream, saturated harvest yellow, deep leaf green, sky blue, and a restrained orange signal.
- Bricolage Grotesque for headings and the wordmark.
- DM Sans for body copy, labels, controls, and navigation.
- DM Mono only for comparable figures such as prices and recommendation scores.
- Large product photography, bold color fields, rounded product frames, and strong section contrast.
- Color photography with factual captions and credits.

## Layout

- One centred container capped at `1440px` with responsive gutters.
- Landing page uses a product-led stage and leaf-green navigation.
- Working screens use clear colorful sections with strong progress and selected states.
- Sections alternate food photography, bold color fields, and compact product information.
- No invented claims, testimonials, certification marks, or decorative metrics.

## Controls

- Primary action: leaf-green or cream pill with high-contrast text.
- Secondary action: transparent pill with a strong green border.
- Choice controls: rounded rectangles selected with deep green fill.
- Focus uses the orange focus token and appears instantly.
- Motion is state-only. No decorative page entrances or hover scaling.

## Copy

- Sentence case and untracked labels.
- No eyebrows or tracked uppercase captions.
- No unsupported nutrition, GI, medical, or product-performance claims.
- The inactive Nutritional-extension candidate stays visible wherever results appear.

## Images

- `Welcome` may use existing rice photography and the Hallmark agriculture stand-in until Arnatattva supplies owned product and field photography.
- The stand-in must remain in a `data-slot` with replacement guidance in the handoff.
- Every library photograph requires its Hallmark-supplied credit.
